## Required Product Boundary

The 21 lessons, their sequence, explanations, activities, resources, rubrics, and progression rules are authored before release. The chatbot is an optional current-page Q&A helper only. It never teaches a separate lesson, selects or creates material, sequences work, grades, estimates mastery, or changes progress.

A separate bounded prose assessor may compare one submitted explanation with
the current authored page and rubric and give formative revision direction. It
cannot author criteria, use outside material, choose remediation, change
progression, or certify mastery. Prose labels never create concept evidence or
satisfy objective lesson completion.
