# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive technical feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for learners and the project.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior
and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behavior
considered inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct and will communicate
moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and when an individual
is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and Privacy

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately by
emailing [aviarchi1994@gmail.com](mailto:aviarchi1994@gmail.com) with the
subject `Trace ML conduct report`. Do not use a public issue or the
vulnerability-reporting channel for conduct reports.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. The maintainer will respect
the privacy and security of the reporter.

## Enforcement Guidelines

The maintainer will use these guidelines to determine consequences:

1. **Correction**: For inappropriate language or unprofessional behavior, the
   maintainer may issue a private written warning and explain the impact.
2. **Warning**: For a repeated or more serious violation, the maintainer may
   restrict interaction for a specified period.
3. **Temporary ban**: For sustained inappropriate behavior, the maintainer may
   prohibit interaction with the project for a specified period.
4. **Permanent ban**: For repeated violations, harassment, aggression, or
   behavior that endangers community members, the maintainer may permanently
   exclude the participant.
